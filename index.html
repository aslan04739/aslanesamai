About

Let me tell you about me

As an SEO professional, my goal is to help businesses succeed online. I offer a range of services, including SEO audits, technical optimization, on-page and off-page SEO, content creation, and backlink acquisition.

I previously worked as an SEO & GEO consultant at Eskimoz, one of France's leading SEO agencies, where I spent a year on technical audits, traffic and performance analysis, and SEO / GEO strategy for large accounts such as Sunelia, Manpower, Engie My Power and Riverly.

I'm also assisting Voices For Animal Welfare with launching their new website and improving its SEO performance, and exploring the impact of Google AI Overviews on search visibility.
