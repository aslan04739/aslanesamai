FAQ

SEO and GEO consulting: FAQ

Who is Aslane Samai?

I'm Aslane Samai, an SEO and GEO consultant based in Paris, France. I help businesses get found on Google and cited in AI answers such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, through technical SEO audits, data analysis and content. I previously spent a year as an SEO & GEO consultant at Eskimoz, one of France's leading SEO agencies, I write the GEO blog aioseo.fr, and I hold certifications in Google Data Analytics, SQL, Technical SEO and SEO management.

What is GEO (generative engine optimization)?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of making a website easy for AI systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to find, understand and cite when they generate answers.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank your pages in search results; GEO aims to get your brand quoted or recommended inside AI-generated answers. They share the same foundations (crawlable, fast, well-structured pages and trustworthy content), but GEO puts more weight on clear, citable facts, structured data and a consistent identity for your brand across the web.

Can you help my website appear in ChatGPT, Gemini or Google AI Overviews?

Yes. I audit how AI crawlers access your site, how your content and brand are understood, and what's missing for you to be cited, then prioritize the fixes. Nobody can guarantee a citation from an AI model, but these are the same levers that improve your Google rankings.

How does the SEO / GEO audit start?

With a 30-minute video call, booked through my calendar. We look at your site together: what is holding back its visibility on Google and in AI answers, what to fix first, and the scope of the audit.

Do you work in French and English?

Yes. I work with clients in both French and English, including SEO content writing in both languages.
