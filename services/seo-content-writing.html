Why it matters

Publishing more doesn't mean ranking more. Content works when it targets a real search intent, covers the topic better than what already ranks, and fits into a clear structure of pages that support each other.

The same qualities make content easy for AI systems to quote: clear answers, precise facts and a structure that's easy to extract.

What's included

01

Keyword research

The queries your audience uses, their volume, their intent and the competition behind them.

02

Semantic clustering

Topics grouped into clusters and pillar pages, so each page has one clear job and pages don't compete.

03

Content briefs

For your writers: intent, structure, questions to answer, entities to cover, internal links and sources.

04

Writing in French and English

Articles and landing pages written for readers first, with search and AI answers in mind.

05

Optimization of existing content

Updating and improving pages that are close to ranking, often the fastest wins.

06

GEO-ready formatting

Definitions, facts, tables and concise answers that AI systems can extract and cite.

How it works

  1. 1
