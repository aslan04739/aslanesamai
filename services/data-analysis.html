Why it matters

On large sites, intuition doesn't scale. Traffic drops hide in page groups, markets or query types, and the interface of the tools can't show them. Data analysis turns thousands of rows into a few clear decisions.

It also answers the questions that matter to the business: what an update really cost, which sections deserve investment, and what traffic to expect next quarter.

What's included

01

Search Console at scale

Full exports through the API, beyond the 1,000-row limit of the interface, by page, query, country and device.

02

Drops and anomalies

What changed, where and when: updates, migrations, cannibalization, seasonality or tracking issues.

03

Segmentation

Traffic by template, section, topic cluster, market or intent, to see what really drives results.

04

Semantic proximity

Embeddings to measure how well each page matches the queries it should rank for, compared with competitors.

05

Traffic forecasting

Forecasts of clicks, impressions and positions with Google's TimesFM and Meta's Prophet.

06

Automation

Recurring analyses automated in Python and SQL, so you get the same answers every month without the manual work.

Questions about this service

What data do you need?

Read access to Search Console, and ideally to Google Analytics 4 or BigQuery exports. I can also work from CSV exports.

Why not just use the Search Console interface?

The interface samples and limits the data. The API and BigQuery give access to much more detail, which you can segment and combine with other sources.

Can you forecast our organic traffic?

Yes, with models such as TimesFM and Prophet, based on your history. Forecasts come with their assumptions and limits.
