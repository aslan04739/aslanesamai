Why it matters

Most sites lose traffic to problems nobody sees: pages Google can't crawl or chooses not to index, content that competes with itself, weak internal linking, slow templates, or authority that never reaches the pages that sell.

An audit finds these problems, measures what each one costs you, and puts them in the order that will move your numbers first. It's the starting point for everything else, including visibility in AI answers.

What's included

01

Crawl and indexing

How Google discovers, crawls and indexes your pages: robots.txt, sitemaps, canonicals, status codes, redirects, JavaScript rendering, crawl budget.

02

Site architecture and internal linking

Depth, orphan pages, link equity and navigation, so your important pages get the attention they need.

03

Performance and Core Web Vitals

LCP, INP and CLS on real templates, with fixes your developers can act on.

04

Semantic coverage

Which queries you rank for, which you miss, cannibalization, and how well each page matches its search intent.

05

Competitive analysis

Where the competitors that matter beat you, on content, authority and features, and where you can win.

06

Authority and backlinks

Your link profile compared with competitors, toxic patterns, and a realistic netlinking plan.

How it works

  1. 1

    Read access to Search Console, Analytics and, when useful, Bing Webmaster Tools. I crawl the site and gather the data.

  3. 3

    Analysis

    I audit, measure and compare you with the competitors that matter, then turn findings into priorities.

  4. 4

    Roadmap and walkthrough

    You get a prioritized action plan, presented live so your team knows exactly what to do and why.

  5. 5

    Follow-up

    I can support implementation with your developers and writers, and track the results over time.

What does an SEO audit include?

How long does an SEO audit take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the site. We agree on the scope and the timeline during the first 30-minute call.
