Why it matters

Generative engines don't show ten blue links. They pick a few sources, quote them and move on. If your content isn't crawlable by AI bots, easy to extract, backed by clear facts and consistent about who you are, someone else gets cited.

Good GEO is good SEO: the foundations are the same. GEO adds what AI systems need on top: entities, citability, structured data, and measurement of where and how often you're actually cited.

Questions about this service

What is GEO?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of making a website easy for AI systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to find, understand and cite when they generate answers.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will cite my site?

No one can guarantee a citation from an AI model. What I can do is remove what prevents it, strengthen the signals that lead to citations, and measure the result.

Do I still need SEO if I do GEO?

Yes. The pages that AI answers cite are mostly pages that already rank well. GEO builds on SEO rather than replacing it.
