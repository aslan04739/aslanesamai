Why it matters

Most SEO reports are screenshots of tools: lots of numbers, few answers. A good dashboard starts from the decisions you need to make, shows the few metrics that matter, and updates itself.

It also makes SEO visible to the rest of the company, which is often what unlocks budget and developer time.

Questions about this service

Looker Studio or Tableau?

Looker Studio is free and connects natively to Google tools, which suits most SEO reporting. Tableau is useful when you already use it or need heavier analysis.

Can the dashboards include paid search and sales data?

Yes. I've built consolidated SEO and SEA reports, and data from your CRM or sales tools can be added when it helps answer your questions.

Who can access the dashboards?

You own them. They can be shared with your team, managers or clients with the access rights you choose.
